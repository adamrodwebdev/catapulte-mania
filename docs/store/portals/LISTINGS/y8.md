## Title
Catapulte Mania

## Short description (one line)
Aim your siege engine and bring down 100 medieval castles — physics, destruction and three engines.

## Description
Bring down the castle, one perfect shot at a time.

Catapulte Mania is a medieval physics game: aim your siege engine, pick the right ammunition and watch towers of wood, stone and marble collapse on their defenders. Every block, barrel and soldier is simulated, so no two collapses are alike.

100 HANDMADE LEVELS
Straw huts and wooden forts first, then stone keeps, iron gates and marble citadels. Moats of water and lava, snowfields, mountains you cannot destroy, crows and wyverns that catch your shots, patrolling guards, armored knights and ogres who swat your boulders away.

THREE SIEGE ENGINES
- Catapult: pull back, aim, release.
- Trebuchet: tap exactly where the shot must land, then choose a high lob or a flat shot.
- Ballista: the ultimate weapon, its bolt goes straight through mountains.

AMMUNITION AND POWERS
Stones, heavy boulders, Greek fire, frost balls, explosive bombs and grapeshot that splits in mid-air. Unlock powers such as the falcon's eye, meteor or earthquake: one per turn, and each use costs points.

MORE TO PLAY
A daily challenge with a link to beat a friend's exact shot, an endless siege, a castle workshop to build and share your own fortress, upgrades, achievements and three difficulty levels. English, French and Indonesian. Dark mode, sound subtitles, high contrast and a trajectory aid.

## Controls / How to play
CATAPULT AND BALLISTA
- Drag back from anywhere on the screen to aim, release to fire
- Hold still (or Shift / a second finger) for fine aiming; release near the start to cancel
- Keyboard: arrow keys to aim, Space or Enter to fire

TREBUCHET
- Tap or click exactly where the projectile must land
- Tap again to freeze the gauge between a high lob and a flat shot

EVERYWHERE
- 1-6: choose ammunition
- Tap or Space during flight: split grapeshot / dive
- P: pause
