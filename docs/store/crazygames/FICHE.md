## Description

Bring down the castle, one perfect shot at a time.

Catapulte Mania is a medieval physics game: aim your siege engine, pick the right ammunition and watch towers of wood, stone and marble collapse on their defenders. Every block, barrel and soldier is simulated, so no two collapses are alike.

**100 handmade levels**
Start with straw huts and wooden forts, then face stone keeps, iron gates and marble citadels. Moats of water and lava, snowfields, mountains you cannot destroy, crows and wyverns that catch your shots, patrolling guards, armored knights and ogres who swat your boulders away.

**Three siege engines**
- **Catapult**: pull back, aim, release.
- **Trebuchet**: tap exactly where the shot must land, then choose a high lob or a flat shot.
- **Ballista**: the ultimate weapon, its bolt goes straight through mountains (unlocked by the best players).

**Ammunition and powers**
Stones, heavy boulders, Greek fire, frost balls (they cross lava and freeze blocks solid), explosive bombs and grapeshot that splits in mid-air. Unlock special powers such as the falcon's eye, meteor or earthquake: one per turn, and each use costs points.

**More to play**
A daily challenge with a share link to beat a friend's exact shot, an endless siege mode, a castle workshop to build and share your own fortress, upgrades, achievements and three difficulty levels.

Playable in English, French and Indonesian, with dark mode, subtitles for sound effects, high contrast and a trajectory aid for players who need it.

## Controls

**Catapult and ballista**
- Drag back from anywhere on the screen to aim (angle and power), release to fire
- Hold still (or Shift / a second finger) for fine aiming; release near the start to cancel
- Keyboard: arrow keys to aim, Space or Enter to fire

**Trebuchet**
- Tap or click exactly where the projectile must land
- Tap again to freeze the gauge between a high lob and a flat shot: the trebuchet fires
- Keyboard: left / right arrows to move the target, Space or Enter to confirm, then again to fire

**Everywhere**
- 1–6: choose ammunition
- Tap or Space during flight: split grapeshot / dive
- P: pause
